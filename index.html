background begone!

begone removes the background from your images, right in your browser.

Your images never leave your device: there is no upload, no account and no tracking. After the first visit, the app also works offline, and you can install it from your browser.

On a plain background, begone also keeps the real shadow of the object, with its colour. You can make the shadow warmer, colder or more diffuse. Use the brushes to fix the result by hand.

The cut-out is done by BiRefNet (MIT license), a segmentation model that runs on your GPU where possible.

Made by Felix Gnass. Free and open source (MIT) on GitHub.
